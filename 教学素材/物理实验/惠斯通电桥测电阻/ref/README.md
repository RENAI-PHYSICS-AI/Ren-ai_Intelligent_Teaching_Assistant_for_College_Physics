# 惠斯通电桥测电阻：参考资料索引

本索引收录 11 项经典原始资料、国家计量机构资料、国际计量指南和大学教学资源。知识库只保存题录、链接与教学性摘要，不在仓库复制受版权保护的全文。访问日期：2026-08-30。

## 1. 使用边界

原始论文用于核对历史归属与平衡思想；NBS/NIST 资料用于核对精密电阻、桥式比较、接触/引线/漏电和校准边界；JCGM 与 NIST 指南用于测量模型和结果表达；OpenStax 与 MIT OCW 用于本科推导。链接失效时按 DOI、报告号或机构题名检索。

## 2. 核心文献

### 2.1 Christie (1833)：桥式平衡的首创

Samuel Hunter Christie, “Experimental determination of the laws of magneto-electric induction in different masses of the same metal, and of its intensity in different metal,” Royal Society archival manuscript, 1833.

- Royal Society archive: https://makingscience.royalsociety.org/items/pt_21_5/
- 用途：历史归属、差动检流与零示思想。

### 2.2 Wheatstone (1843)：系统推广电阻天平

Charles Wheatstone, “The Bakerian Lecture—An Account of Several New Instruments and Processes for Determining the Constants of a Voltaic Circuit,” *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London*, 133 (1843), 303–327. DOI: 10.1098/rstl.1843.0014.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1098/rstl.1843.0014
- JSTOR: https://www.jstor.org/stable/i206998
- 用途：原始桥路、Ohm 定律背景与电阻比较。

### 2.3 Maxwell (1873)：经典电磁学中的电桥

James Clerk Maxwell, *A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism*, Vol. I, Clarendon Press, 1873，相关章节 “Wheatstone’s Bridge”.

- Wikisource: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Treatise_on_Electricity_and_Magnetism/Volume_1
- Library of Congress: https://www.loc.gov/item/03015568/
- 用途：网络方程、比率测量和经典理论脉络。

### 2.4 Mueller (1916/1917)：电阻温度计电桥

E. F. Mueller, “Wheatstone Bridges and Some Accessory Apparatus for Resistance Thermometry,” *Bulletin of the Bureau of Standards*, Vol. 13, p. 547 ff.

- NIST PDF: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/bulletin/13/nbsbulletinv13n4p547_A2b.pdf
- 用途：接触电阻、引线、换向和三线/电位端结构。

### 2.5 Wenner & Smith (1923)：低电阻测量边界

Frank Wenner and Alva Smith, “Measurement of Low Resistance by Means of the Wheatstone Bridge,” *Scientific Papers of the Bureau of Standards*, 19 (1923), 297–306, S481. DOI: 10.6028/nbsscipaper.158.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.6028/nbsscipaper.158
- NIST index: https://www.nist.gov/nist-research-library/scientific-papers-bureau-standards-volume-19
- 用途：低电阻时引线与接点限制。

### 2.6 Thomas (1948)：精密电阻与电桥校准

James L. Thomas, *Precision Resistors and Their Measurement*, NBS Circular 470, 1948. DOI: 10.6028/NBS.CIRC.470.

- NIST record: https://www.nist.gov/publications/circular-bureau-standards-no-470precision-resistors-and-their-measurement
- PDF: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/circ/nbscircular470.pdf
- 用途：灵敏度与准确度、温度、自热、接触误差和校准。

### 2.7 Elmquist et al. (2004)：NIST 直流标准电阻服务

R. E. Elmquist et al., *NIST Measurement Service for DC Standard Resistors*, NIST Technical Note 1458, 2004.

- NIST record: https://www.nist.gov/publications/tech-note-1458nist-measurement-service-dc-standard-resistors
- PDF: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/TN/nbstechnicalnote1458.pdf
- 用途：量值溯源、比率桥、四端标准、守卫与量程边界。

### 2.8 OpenStax (2022)：本科零示法

OpenStax, *College Physics 2e*, Sec. 21.5 “Null Measurements”.

- Official page: https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/21-5-null-measurements
- 用途：平衡式推导、无法达到绝对零电流以及桥臂不确定度。

### 2.9 MIT OCW (2006)：戴维南与测量仪器

MIT OpenCourseWare 6.071J, “Linear Circuit Analysis,” 2006.

- Official resource: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-071j-introduction-to-electronics-signals-and-measurement-spring-2006/resources/linear_crct_ana/
- 用途：未平衡桥端口等效、检流计负载和灵敏度。

### 2.10 JCGM 100:2008：测量不确定度

Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology, *Evaluation of Measurement Data—Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement*, JCGM 100:2008(E). DOI: 10.59161/JCGM100-2008E.

- BIPM index: https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/guides
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E
- 用途：输入量、灵敏度系数、协方差、合成不确定度与报告。

### 2.11 Taylor & Kuyatt (1994)：NIST 不确定度指南

B. N. Taylor and C. E. Kuyatt, *Guidelines for Evaluating and Expressing the Uncertainty of NIST Measurement Results*, NIST Technical Note 1297, 1994. DOI: 10.6028/NIST.TN.1297.

- NIST document: https://www.nist.gov/document/tn1297spdf
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.1297
- 用途：A 类/B 类评定、覆盖因子和标准化报告。

## 3. 推荐阅读顺序

先读 OpenStax 和 /principle 建立平衡方程，再读 MIT OCW 和 /sensitivity 理解戴维南灵敏度；随后读 Thomas 与 NIST TN 1458 识别接触、自热、守卫和量程边界；最后用 JCGM 100 与 TN 1297 完成不确定度预算。历史部分按 Christie—Wheatstone—Maxwell 阅读。
